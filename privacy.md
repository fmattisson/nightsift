# NightSift privacy policy

*Last updated: 13 November 2026*

NightSift is an app by Fredrik Mattisson that records sounds during the night, such as
snoring or talking in your sleep, and keeps only the parts where there is sound.

## The short version

NightSift collects no personal data. Everything it records stays on your phone.

## Details

- **Microphone.** NightSift uses the microphone only while you have started a recording, while
  you run the sensitivity test, and for half a second when you press Start, to check that the
  microphone works. While it records, Android shows a notification and the microphone
  indicator. During the sensitivity test and the check at Start, nothing is saved.
- **Motion sensor.** While recording, NightSift reads the phone's motion sensor (accelerometer)
  only to notice when the phone is picked up, so the noise of handling it is not saved. The
  readings are used on the spot and not stored.
- **What is stored.** For each night: the short sound files that were above your chosen
  sensitivity, a file with the loudness for each second of the night, and a small file with
  times, settings, names you gave and the sounds you marked with a heart. These are stored in
  the app's own folder on your phone (`Android/data/io.github.fmattisson.nightsift`). They are
  deleted when you delete them in the app, when the app's "Delete old recordings" option removes
  them, or when you uninstall the app.
- **Saved copies.** If you use "Save to phone", copies of the chosen sounds are written to the
  phone's shared Recordings folder (`Recordings/NightSift`). Like any file there, other apps
  that may read your audio files can see them, and they stay after NightSift is uninstalled
  until you delete them.
- **No internet.** The app has no permission to use the internet. It cannot send anything
  anywhere. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
- **No backups.** NightSift's recordings and settings are excluded from Android's cloud backup
  and from phone-to-phone transfer.
- **Sharing.** Recordings only leave your phone if you share them yourself, using the app's
  Share buttons. The app or service you share to has its own privacy policy.
- **Children.** NightSift is not directed at children.
- **Other people.** If you record a room where other people sleep or talk, let them know.

## Not a medical device

NightSift does not diagnose snoring, sleep apnea or any other condition.
